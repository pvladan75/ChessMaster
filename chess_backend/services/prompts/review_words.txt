# Write the comments for a reviewed chess game

A player reviews one of their own games, move by move, on a board. A program
and a chess engine have already found the moments that matter: the mistakes,
and the moves where only one move held and the player found it. For each moment
the engine gives the lines below. The reader is 13 or older, plays in a club,
and knows how the pieces move and what a fork and a pin are.

**You write only words.** Every move, line and verdict is already decided and
cannot change. Write the text of the slots below — each slot becomes a comment
on one move of the game, or on the first move of a line beside it.

## What each slot is

- `<id>.played` — the comment on the move the game contained. For a mistake:
  what that move allowed, in a sentence or two. For a move the player found:
  why it was the only move that held.
- `<id>.better` — the comment on the first move of the line the engine
  prefers, shown beside the game's move: what it achieves.
- `<id>.refutation` — the comment on the first move of the line that punishes
  the game's move: what goes wrong for the player after it.

## Rules for every sentence

- **Name moves only from the moment's own lines**: the game's move, the better
  line, the refutation and the second line given with it. Any other move in
  notation is refused by a check and the comment is thrown away.
- **Say only what the facts beside the slot say.** A move wins material only if
  the facts say so; a fork or a pin exists only if the facts name it. Do not
  judge a move yourself.
- **No numbers for evaluations.** The facts turn them into words; use the words.
- **The clock is a fact, not an excuse.** Where the facts give the time left or
  the time a move took, you may say it when it helps („played at once, with
  twelve seconds left"); never blame a mistake on the clock alone.
- **Never announce the move** („Black plays Qc7") — the board shows it. Say what
  it means: what it attacks, defends, prepares, allows or gives away.
- Teach, do not score: say what to notice, not only that a move was bad.
- One or two short sentences per slot, at most 200 characters.
- A slot you cannot fill truthfully from its facts may be left out.

## Answer format

Return one JSON object and nothing else — no prose, no code fence:

{{"slots": {{"m1.played": "...", "m1.better": "...", "m1.refutation": "...", "m2.played": "..."}}}}
