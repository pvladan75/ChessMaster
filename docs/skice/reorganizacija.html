Today, for comparison

Four tabs. The Library tab holds two tools and a Windows-only card; the Analysis bar holds thirteen actions, four of them tutorial doors; the room's left column is a library, a setup dialog and an editor in one.

What every variant changes — Analysis and the room

One door in Analysis, the questions behind it. The room's column split into what it is: the board's actions and the library.

Variant A — same four tabs, one door per job

The foundation only. The trainer panel moves from People to Sessions; the Library tab becomes a tools row and one list of everything you keep; nothing else moves.

Variant B — tabs by what you want to do recommended

Home · Practise · Analyse · Teach, and Settings. Home is built from data: the blocks it shows depend on whether anyone teaches you and whether you teach anyone. Teach holds the tutorials, Preparation, sessions, students and the library. Analyse is the board itself.

Question 4 of the plan: People merged into Teach and Home (drawn here), or kept as a fifth destination. Four tabs plus Settings is what fits the landscape rail at 360 dp without the cut that item 174 fixed.

Variant C — the app knows whether you teach

Variant B with two tab lists. teaches is true when the user has an accepted student, has saved a tutorial, or switched „Show teaching tools" on in Settings. Home is built once and both shells use it.

The Tutorial Studio on a phone (§7 of the plan)

One controller, two layouts, one part at a time. The board and the move strip stay pinned; under them a three-way strip — Line, Task, Parts — holds what the desktop shows in panels. Landscape uses the layout every board screen got on 16.9.2026.

What the phone gives up: the whole flow at a glance, two panes, keyboard shortcuts. What it gets back: fixing a comment on the bus, adding one part from a position saved in Preparation, and one app on both devices.
