Last changed: {{LAST_UPDATED}}

This policy covers Chess Brain Trainer only. The other apps on this site are separate products with their own policies, because they handle different data.

1. Who processes your data

The data controller is {{OPERATOR_NAME}}, {{OPERATOR_ADDRESS}}, Republic of Serbia. Contact for privacy questions: {{PRIVACY_EMAIL}}.

2. The short version

Chess Brain Trainer requires no account, shows no advertising, and collects no personal data such as your name, email address or contacts. There is nothing to sign in to and nothing to sign out of.

3. What stays on your device

Everything the app records about your practice — puzzles played, results, XP, ranks, achievements and settings — is stored only on your device. It never leaves it, we never see it, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app.

4. Crash reports

The app uses Firebase Crashlytics (Google) to report crashes and errors. A report contains the device model, the Android and app version, the technical details of the crash, and a randomly generated installation identifier — which is how two crashes can be recognised as coming from the same installation. It does not contain your name, email address or anything you typed.

Reports are sent over an encrypted connection, are used only to find and fix faults, and are deleted automatically after 90 days.

5. Purchases

The optional Premium purchase is processed by Google Play. Google is the seller and handles your payment — we never receive and never store a card number or any payment detail.

6. What we do not do

  • We show no advertising and use no advertising identifiers.
  • We do not sell or share data with advertisers.
  • We do not build profiles.
  • The app contains no links to external content.

7. Your rights

You can delete everything the app has recorded about you at any time by uninstalling it — that data is on your device and nowhere else.

For crash reports, which are the only data that leaves your device, you may ask what is held, ask for it to be deleted, or object to the processing, by writing to {{PRIVACY_EMAIL}}. We answer within 30 days. Please say roughly when the crash happened and on which device, because a report carries no name to search by.

If you believe your rights have been infringed, you may address the Commissioner for Information of Public Importance and Personal Data Protection of the Republic of Serbia.

8. Children

The app is suitable for all ages. It shows no advertising, asks for nothing about the person using it, and links to no external content. It is not directed at children specifically, and it collects no more from a young user than from anybody else — which is nothing beyond the crash reports above.

If we change this policy, the new version appears at this same address and the date at the top changes.
