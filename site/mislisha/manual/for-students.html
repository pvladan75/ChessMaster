Work with your trainer

If somebody teaches you through Mislisha, this is your side of it: the live lesson, the work they set you, and the positions that come back later.

Be connected first

In the People tab, either you send the request — I am a student, then Trainer's email and Send request — or you answer theirs in the bell at the top, Notifications and Invitations, with Accept. Nothing is shared until one of you accepts.

Join a live session

Your trainer gives you a code. In the Sessions tab, type it into Enter room code (e.g. 123456) and press Join. If the session was arranged in advance, the invitation is in your notifications with Go to session when the time comes.

In the room you share one board. To speak, press Raise hand to speak. If you have something on your own board worth showing, Show my position to trainer. You can leave and come back: the band at the top of the app holds the room, and Resume session returns to it.

The work your trainer sets

It arrives under My assignments in the Sessions tab, and on your own My Assignments screen, where Overdue marks anything past its day. With nothing set yet it says You have no assigned drills.

Walking a tutorial

A tutorial is a series of positions with something said about each. Two ways through it:

  • Play tutorial reads it to you and plays the moves at the speed of the reading, stopping wherever you have something to do. Stop reading ends that.
  • The buttons under the board walk it move by move, and Next part and Previous part move between its parts.

Where the tutorial asks something, the board waits for your move or offers the answers to choose from. Correct. is what you are after; Show me gives it away when you are stuck. Where a line splits, it asks which way to go: Multiple lines continue from here — which one? If you have pushed the pieces around while thinking, the app says Trying moves — nothing is graded here. and Reset position puts the position back. Finish closes it at the end.

If nothing is read aloud

The tutorial says which language it is written in, and it is never read in another one. Where your device has no voice for that language the app says so — No voice on this device — navigate with buttons. — and the tutorial works exactly as well with the buttons.

What comes back

Positions you have met return on their own, days later, under Review in the Sessions tab. For each one you say How well did you remember?, and that decides when you see it again. Show description and Show continuation help when it will not come. When there is nothing due: Nothing is due.

Your trainer can leave you remarks on a finished assignment, under Review and comments.

What can go wrong

  • Answer not sent — check your connection. — your answer did not reach the server; it is not lost, try again when you are back online.
  • This tutorial is no longer available. — your trainer deleted it after setting it.
  • A button asks you to sign in — you are using the app as a guest; joining a session needs an account.
