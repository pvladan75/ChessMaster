Work with your trainer

If somebody teaches you through Mislisha, this is your side of it: the live lesson, the work and the videos they set you, and what they say about it afterwards.

Be connected first

On the Home tab, either you send the request — I am a student, then Trainer's email and Send a request — or you answer theirs in the bell at the top, Notifications and Invitations, with Accept. Nothing is shared until one of you accepts.

Join a live session

There is no code to type. When your trainer is in a session, the Home tab shows In a session now with their name; press Join. If your trainer invited you, the invitation also pops up, and it stays in your notifications with Join for as long as that session is running; once it is over the invitation says This session has ended.

In the room you share one board. To speak, press Raise hand to speak. If you have something on your own board worth showing, Show my position to trainer. You can leave and come back: the band at the top of the app holds the room, and Resume session returns to it.

The work your trainer sets

It arrives under Set for me on the Home tab, and on your own My Assignments screen, where Overdue marks anything past its day. With nothing set yet it says You have no assigned drills.

A tutorial video

A tutorial your trainer sends you is a video. Open it from My Assignments and press Download video: it opens in your browser and is saved to your device, where you watch it like any other video. Once the whole file has arrived the assignment counts as done, and your trainer sees that you downloaded it — not whether you watched it. Download again fetches it once more whenever you need it.

If your trainer has deleted the tutorial since, the screen says so: This video is no longer available. Ask your trainer to send it again.

Afterwards

Your trainer can leave you remarks on a finished assignment, under Review and comments.

Past sessions you can rewatch are listed under Recordings on Home.

What can go wrong

  • This video is no longer available. Ask your trainer to send it again. — your trainer deleted the tutorial after sending it.
  • A button asks you to sign in — you are using the app as a guest; joining a session needs an account.
