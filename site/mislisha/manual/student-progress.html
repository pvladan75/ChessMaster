See what a student did

One screen per student: how they are doing, what you have set them, and what they have finished. It is also where you set the next thing.

Open it

In the People tab, on an accepted student's row, press Progress and assignments.

What it shows

  • Overview — their rating, how much they have solved, how accurate they were, and how many days they turned up. The Period button at the top chooses what „recently" means: Last 7 days, Last 30 days or Last 90 days.
  • By theme — where they are weak. With too little solved to say anything yet, it says so rather than guessing.
  • Assignments — everything you have set, and how far they have got. With nothing set it says You have not assigned any drills to this student yet.

Set the next thing from here

Assign tutorial and Assign drill are on this screen, so you can look at what is weak and set the answer to it without going anywhere else — see sending a tutorial. To take one back, Withdraw assignment.

A report for a parent

The Parent report button at the top makes a page a parent can read without an account in the app. Choose the Period it covers, add a Note for parent (optional) if you want to say something in your own words, and press Create. When it is ready — Report ready — you can Open it yourself or use Copy link and send the link to the parent.

Review: what comes back

Positions a student has met return later, spaced out over time, in the Review queue on their own Sessions tab. For each one the student answers How well did you remember?, and that answer decides how long it is before they see it again. Show description and Show continuation are there when they are stuck. With nothing due it says Nothing is due. or Done for today.

What can go wrong

  • The screen says Report not available. — there is nothing to report on yet for that period.
  • The parent's link will not open — use Copy link and send it by mail instead.
  • A grade is not kept — the student's device says Grade not saved — check your connection., and the position stays due.
