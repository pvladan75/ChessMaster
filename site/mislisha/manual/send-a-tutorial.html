Send a tutorial to a student

A tutorial you have saved does nothing until somebody is given it. There are two ways to hand one over, and they end in the same place: the student's list of assignments.

It can only go to a student who has accepted your invitation. Until then the list of people to send to says You have no students who have accepted the invitation. — see connecting with your students.

From your list of tutorials

  1. Open the Library tab and, on the Interactive tutorials card, press Saved tutorials.
  2. Find the tutorial — Search by name narrows a long list.
  3. Press Send to student on its row and choose the student.

The app says Tutorial sent to student. when it has gone.

From the student

The other way round, when you are looking at one student rather than at your tutorials: open them from the People tab and press Assign tutorial. Here you can add more than the tutorial itself:

  • Tutorial — which one. Nothing is sent until one is chosen; the dialog answers Select a tutorial. otherwise.
  • Note for student (optional) — a sentence that travels with it.
  • Set due date — a day it should be done by, and Remove due date if you change your mind.

Assign sends it. The same screen also has Assign drill for puzzles rather than a tutorial.

What the student sees

It appears in their Sessions tab under My assignments, and in their own My Assignments screen, with Overdue marked if the day has passed. Walking a tutorial is the same on a phone as on a computer.

Taking one back

On the student's page, Withdraw assignment removes it. It asks first — Withdraw assignment? — because it takes with it whatever the student has already done on it.

What can go wrong

  • The student is not in the list. They have not accepted yet, or a parent's confirmation is still outstanding.
  • There is nothing to send. The dialog says you have no saved tutorials and points at the studio — write one first.
  • The student opens it and it is gone. This tutorial is no longer available. means the tutorial itself was deleted after it was assigned.
