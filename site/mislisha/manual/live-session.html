Run a live session

A session is you and your student in a room at the same time, on one board, with voice. It is the live half of the app; a tutorial is the half a student walks alone.

Start one

In the Teach tab, on the New session card, press Start to open a room with you leading it. Nobody types a code: your students see In a session now with your name on their Home tab and press Join. Only your own students can come in. You must be signed in; a guest is taken to the sign-in screen first.

Only one session at a time. Starting a new one ends the one you had open, for everybody in it. If you are still in somebody else's session, the app asks Leave the other session? first.

Call your students in

From inside the room, Invite students to session sends an invitation that pops up on the student's screen. Tick names one by one, or tap a group to tick everybody in it.

Inside the room

Everybody sees one board. The trainer moves on it, and students too once the trainer turns on Students may move. The left column has two parts, Board and Library:

  • Voice. Nobody's voice is on until they press Turn on voice themselves. While somebody is talking and yours is off, a strip under the room's bar names them and offers Join voice. A student asks to speak with Raise hand to speak; the trainer has Mute all students, and Leave voice drops the audio without leaving the room.
  • Drawing. Arrows and coloured squares go on the board for both of you, with Undo arrow and Clear all arrows.
  • The board. Set up position, Import PGN, and Save position put specific positions on the board or save them; paste a FEN string into the FEN field to load one.
  • The library in the room. The room's list is the same library as on the Teach tab, narrowed to Tutorials and Positions, with Mine and From trainer beside them, the labels you gave your positions, and a search field that reads titles and labels alike. Tapping a row puts it on the board; a tutorial starts from its first part. A row's Edit tutorial opens it in the Tutorial Studio.
  • Carrying on elsewhere. Export to Analysis 🔬 hands the position to the analysis screen.
  • Who may do what. Room access narrows who can come in, Students may move opens or locks the board for everybody but you, and Allow Stockfish for student says whether the student may ask the engine. Whoever started the session leads it; that does not change while it runs. A student can offer their own board back with Show my position to trainer.

Leaving, and coming back

Whoever started the session has End session, which ends it for everybody and sends them back to Home with The session has ended.; invitations to it stop working. Everybody else has Leave session, which ends only their part of it; Stay in room is the way out of that question if you pressed it by mistake. If you simply walk away — another tab, the back button, closing the app — a band stays across the top of the app with the room's code in it, and Resume session puts you back on the board.

Recording

A lesson is never recorded. Recording is offered only while you are alone in the room, which is how a trainer makes their own material: the button refuses with Audio is only recorded while you are alone in the room. and, if somebody joins while you are recording, it stops.

Alone, Start recording begins and the room shows Recording in progress. Stop and save or Finish and save recording ends it and keeps it. What you have made appears under Recordings on the Home tab and in the Library on Teach. On that screen the spacebar plays and pauses.

What can go wrong

  • The invitation has no Join — it says This session has ended.: the trainer ended that session or started another. Look at In a session now on Home, or ask to be invited again.
  • Nobody hears anybody — read the voice panel first: a voice that did not start says why in red above the button, and a microphone that does not work says so in the panel. Then check that the microphone is allowed for the app in the operating system, and that neither side has pressed Leave voice.
  • The student cannot move — a new session starts with the board locked; the trainer turns on Students may move.
