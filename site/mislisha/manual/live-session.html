Run a live session

A session is you and your student in a room at the same time, on one board, with voice. It is the live half of the app; a tutorial is the half a student walks alone.

Start one

In the Sessions tab, New Session opens a room with you as host and gives it a code. Your student joins by typing that code into Enter room code (e.g. 123456) and pressing Join — the field is on the same tab, on their device. You must be signed in; a guest is taken to the sign-in screen first.

Only one session at a time: starting a second one while a first is open brings up You already have an active session.

Invite instead of dictating a code

Schedule a Session arranges one for later: a time, a Description (optional), and, under Invite friends to scheduled session:, the students to invite. Schedule and Save keeps it, and Add to Google Calendar puts it in your calendar. Whoever you invited gets a Session Invitation in their notifications, with Go to session when its time comes.

From inside the room, Invite friends to session does the same for a session already running.

Inside the room

Both of you see one board, and either of you can move on it. What is around it:

  • Voice. A student asks to speak with Raise hand to speak; the trainer has Mute all students, and Leave voice drops the audio without leaving the room.
  • Drawing. Arrows and coloured squares go on the board for both of you, with Undo arrow and Clear all arrows.
  • The position. Set up position (Board Setup), Load FEN, and Import PGN (file or text) put something specific on the board; Save current position keeps it in your library.
  • Your material. The room's list of positions and tutorials is filtered by Mine, From trainer and All, and Load onto board puts one up.
  • Carrying on elsewhere. Export to Analysis 🔬 hands the position to the analysis screen, and Create tutorial (multiple positions) starts a tutorial from what is on the board.
  • Who may do what. Room access decides who can come in, Change role swaps host and guest, and Allow Stockfish for student says whether the student may ask the engine. A student can offer their own board back with Show my position to trainer.

Leaving, and coming back

Leave session ends your part of it; Stay in room is the way out of that question if you pressed it by mistake. If you simply walk away — another tab, the back button, closing the app — a band stays across the top of the app with the room's code in it, and Resume session puts you back on the board.

Recording

A lesson is never recorded. Recording is offered only while you are alone in the room, which is how a trainer makes their own material: the button refuses with Audio is only recorded while you are alone in the room. and, if somebody joins while you are recording, it stops.

Alone, Start recording begins and the room shows Recording in progress. Stop and save or Finish and save recording ends it and keeps it, and what you have made appears under Recorded material in the Sessions tab. On that screen the spacebar plays and pauses.

What can go wrong

  • The code does not work — it belongs to a session that has been left, or it was mistyped; ask for it again, or invite by name instead.
  • Nobody hears anybody — check that the microphone is allowed for the app in the operating system, and that neither side has pressed Leave voice.
  • The student cannot move — roles decide who leads; Change role swaps them.
