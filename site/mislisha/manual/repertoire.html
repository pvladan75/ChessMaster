Build an opening repertoire

A repertoire here is not a set of puzzles to solve. It is a set of decisions you make, one position at a time — what you would play — kept so you can rehearse them later and see what is still missing.

Start one

Training tab → Opening repertoire → Open repertoire. New repertoire begins one: you say which side you are preparing, White or Black, and which opening — Choose opening, or Paste position (FEN) to start from a particular position rather than from move one.

Decide, position by position

In Build, the board shows a position your side has to answer, and you play the move you would play. What the app adds to that choice:

  • Ask engine — what Stockfish thinks of it.
  • Open book (1 query) — what has been played here, asked once rather than continuously, and Opponent rating says whose games to count.
  • Ask AI about position — a sentence about the ideas in the position.
  • Comment on position — your own note, which is what you will read when you meet the position again; Add to my comment keeps what was suggested.

For the opponent's replies you choose how much to prepare — How many replies to prepare — and Prepare reply takes one on. Do not prepare this puts a line aside deliberately, so that „not prepared" and „not decided yet" stay different things. Suggest main line proposes a continuation when you want one, and Continue line goes on down it.

See what is missing

See what is not prepared is the honest view of a repertoire: the positions your side can be asked and has no answer for. Show entire repertoire and Show only from this position choose how much of it to look at, and Review unconfirmed collects the decisions that were made automatically and never confirmed by you.

Rehearse it

Drill plays the opponent's side and asks you for yours, from your own decisions rather than from a database. Drill branch and Drill this branch narrow it to the part you are working on, Skip rehearsal passes a position you know, and Next position moves on.

Positions you have drilled come back later on their own, spaced out over time, in Review on the Sessions tab.

Tidying up

  • Extract into new opening — split a branch off as a repertoire of its own.
  • Delete moves from database and Delete your decisions? — removing lines, with the question asked first, because a decision deleted is work lost.
  • Restore this branch — what to press when a branch was cut off by mistake.
