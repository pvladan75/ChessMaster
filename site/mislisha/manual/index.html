Mislisha user manual

Find the thing you want to do. Each page says where it is in the app and how to do it, in the words the app itself uses.

The app has four tabs — Training, Sessions, Library and People — and Settings. On Windows they are down the left side; on a phone, along the bottom. Press F1 on Windows for the keyboard shortcuts.

Start here

  • Find your way around Signing in, the four tabs, the top bar, and what is in Settings.

If you teach

  • Connect with your students or your trainer Sending and answering a request, what the waiting states mean, groups, and ending a relationship.
  • Write a tutorial A lesson a student walks through on their own: a line played on the board, what you say about it, and questions.
  • Send a tutorial to a student The two ways to hand one over, with a note and a due date — and how to take it back.
  • See what a student did Progress, assignments, the review queue, and the report a parent can be sent.
  • Run a live session A room with one board and voice: starting it, inviting, and what is kept afterwards.
  • Prepare material: your library and positions from a book Your own board without a student, saved positions, and diagrams read out of a chess book in PDF.
  • Turn a tutorial into a video Your own narration or a synthesised voice, and the export that draws the film.

If you practise on your own

  • Practise on your own Your games and mistakes, tactics, mates, endgames and winning positions.
  • Build an opening repertoire Deciding what you would play, position by position, and rehearsing it.
  • Analyse a position or a game The engine, the opening database, the variation tree — and how a line becomes a tutorial.

If you have a trainer

  • Work with your trainer Joining a session, doing what is set you, and what comes back for review.

For parents

  • For parents What you are asked to confirm, what the app keeps, and the report a trainer can send you.
