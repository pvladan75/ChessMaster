Chess Brain Trainer

Chess puzzles that train calculation and pattern recognition, on your own and at your own pace. Published on Google Play.

What it does

  • Puzzles sorted by theme and difficulty, with XP, ranks and achievements.
  • Everything you solve stays on your device.
  • An optional Premium purchase, sold through Google Play.
No account, no advertising. There is nothing to sign in to, and the app asks for nothing about the person using it.
