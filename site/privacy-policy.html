2. Who this policy applies to

This is a chess coaching app. It is used by trainers and students, and a significant share of the students are children. If you are under 15, an account may be opened only with the consent of a parent or guardian — see section 8.

3.3 The record of a lesson, and recordings

A lesson is kept as a timeline of moves, arrows and marks on the board, together with the list of participants. That record can be replayed later.

The interaction between a trainer and a student is not recorded. No participant's voice is captured or kept — under anybody's consent. Replaying a lesson is silent.

Audio exists in one case only: an adult user who is alone in the room may record their own material, for example a video lesson, then download it and publish it wherever they like. The server refuses audio as soon as anybody else is in the room, and stops the recording if somebody joins.

3.4 Payment data

Subscriptions are charged through Google Play. Google is the seller and processes your payment data — we never see and never store a card number or any payment detail. From Google we receive only the purchase identifier, the product name, the subscription status and the expiry date.

3.5 Resource usage data

We record, in aggregate per account and per month: seconds of voice communication, number of rendered video files, number of AI comments requested and number of pages scanned. We use this only to know what the service costs us and to prevent abuse.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not use it for advertising and do not pass it to advertisers.
  • We do not build profiles for marketing purposes.
  • We do not send your name, email or any personal data to an artificial intelligence service (see 5.2).

5. Who the data is passed to

We use the following service providers. Each receives only what it needs.

5.1 Agora (voice communication)

Carries live audio during a lesson. Agora processes the audio stream and technical connection data. The transfer takes place outside Serbia.

5.2 Google Gemini (AI explanations of moves)

When you request an AI comment, we send chess data only: the position (FEN), the engine evaluation, the move classification and pre-calculated tactical and positional findings. We do not send your name, email, account identifier or any other personal data — Google cannot tell from that request who you are.

5.3 Google Play (billing) and Google sign-in

See 3.4. Google sign-in is used only to confirm identity.

5.4 Lichess and chess evaluation services

For position analysis we send the position (FEN) only, without any data about the user.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access your data, to ask for correction of inaccurate data, to ask for deletion, to restrict processing, to object to processing and to transfer your data to another controller.

Send a request to {{PRIVACY_EMAIL}}. We answer within 30 days.

If you believe your rights have been infringed, you may address the Commissioner for Information of Public Importance and Personal Data Protection of the Republic of Serbia.

8. Children

A significant share of our users are children. Therefore:

  • An account for a child under 15 may be opened only with the consent of a parent or guardian.
  • A lesson is not recorded. A child's voice is never captured, not even with a parent's consent — no such option exists. Audio can be recorded only by an adult user who is alone in the room.
  • A parent may at any time ask to see the data kept about the child and to have it deleted, by writing to {{PRIVACY_EMAIL}}.
  • We show no advertising to children and use their data for nothing except the coaching itself and the report to their trainer and parent.

What consent looks like and what it covers is described on the Parent consent page.

9. Security

Passwords are stored as a bcrypt hash. Communication with the server goes over HTTPS. Access to a student's data is held by only the trainer the student is connected to — no other trainer can see another trainer's students.

No measure is absolute. In the event of a data breach that may put your rights at risk, we will inform you and the Commissioner as the law requires.

10. Changes

If we change this policy, we publish the new version at this same address and change the date at the top. We inform you by email of significant changes.
