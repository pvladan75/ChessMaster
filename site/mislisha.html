Mislisha

A chess coaching platform: a live lesson with a shared board and voice, homework, progress tracking and a report for the parent — an app for the work of a chess trainer.

In preparation for release. This page carries the documents that have to be publicly available; the full description and the download arrive with the release.

What it does

  • A live lesson in a room: a shared board, voice, arrows and marks.
  • A lesson can be replayed afterwards, as a timeline of moves and marks. A lesson is never recorded with sound — see the privacy policy.
  • Puzzles and homework a trainer assigns, with results per position.
  • Spaced repetition, progress tracking and a report for the parent.
