Mislisha

A chess coaching platform: lessons a student works through on their own, live lessons on a shared board with voice, practice for players without a trainer, and the record of what a student has actually done.

In preparation for release. This page carries the documents that have to be publicly available, and the user manual; the download arrives with the release.

What it does

  • Tutorials. A trainer writes a lesson — a line on the board, a sentence about each position, arrows and marks, and questions along the way — and a student walks it alone, at their own pace, with the text read aloud in the language it was written in.
  • Live lessons. A room with one shared board, voice, arrows and marks. A lesson is never recorded with sound — see the privacy policy.
  • Homework and review. Tutorials and puzzles a trainer sets, with results per position, and spaced repetition that brings a position back before it is forgotten.
  • Practice on your own. Your own games and the mistakes in them, tactics matched to your rating, mates, endgames from master games, and an opening repertoire you build decision by decision.
  • Analysis. Stockfish on your own machine, an opening database, and a tree of the variations you try.
  • Video. A tutorial can be exported as a film, narrated by a synthesised voice or by the trainer's own recording.
  • For the parent. A trainer can send a report of what a child has done — no account needed to read it.
