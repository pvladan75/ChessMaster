Blindfold Trainer

Practice holding the board in your head: moves without a board, from a single square to a whole game.

In preparation for release.

What it does

  • Exercises that build up from naming squares to following a whole game without seeing it.
  • Progress and settings stay on your device.
Fully offline. The app holds no internet permission at all, so nothing it records can leave your device.
